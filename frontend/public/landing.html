FastConsignee is a point of sale for consignment shops. The buyer pays once, and each consignor’s share and the shop’s commission arrive in one Solana transaction.

  • One transaction for every share

Your shop, your rules.

Consignment deals are never identical. FastConsignee keeps each rule where it belongs, so the split is right without a calculator.

  • Commission per item

    Each item carries its own rate, not one rate for the whole shop.

  • Refund deductions

    The shop refunds first, and the consignor’s part comes off their next sales.

  • Who carries a discount

    The shop or the consignor, chosen for each line.

    ShopConsignor

  • Locked exchange rate

    The Rupiah total is held at one SOL rate while the QR is active.

    Locked

  • Held stock

    An open order holds its items until it is paid or expires.

  • QR time limit

    Five minutes, then a two-minute grace period before stock is released.

    5 min

  • Up to five consignors

    One cart, one transaction, up to five consignors plus the shop.

  • Signed wallets

    A wallet is linked only after its owner signs a one-time message.

Questions, answered plainly.

Everything on this page describes what runs today. Read the code to check it.

Is this real money?

No. FastConsignee runs on Solana Devnet, where SOL has no monetary value. Prices appear in Rupiah and convert to Devnet SOL at a rate locked while the QR is active.

What does a buyer need?

A Solana wallet set to Devnet, such as Phantom, holding some Devnet SOL. They can scan the QR with a phone, or open the payment page and connect a browser wallet.

What happens if a payment fails?

All transfers sit in one transaction, so they succeed or fail together. If it fails, no balance changes, the held stock is released and the cashier makes a new QR.

How do refunds work?

The owner refunds one item from the shop wallet to the wallet that paid. The consignor’s part is recorded as a deduction and comes off their next sales.

Can consignors check their own sales?

Yes. Each consignor has a dashboard of their sales and deductions, with links to the transactions on Solana Explorer.

Who can sign in?

There is no open sign-up yet. Only accounts that already have a role in a shop can sign in.

Can a shop in Indonesia use it today?

Not for real payments. Bank Indonesia prohibits crypto as a means of payment, so this version stays on Devnet.
